Simple Workflow

How ReviewPilot works

Go from raw feedback to thoughtful responses in three straightforward steps.

1

Paste your reviews

Copy multiple customer reviews from Google Maps, Yelp, TripAdvisor, or delivery platforms and paste them directly into the analyzer, one review per line.

2

AI analyzes & drafts replies

Our intelligent engine scans each review in parallel, determines sentiment, detects topic categories, and drafts a polite response matching your preferred tone.

3

Review, copy & alert

Copy replies to your clipboard with one click, tweak drafts inline if desired, and receive immediate Telegram pings whenever high-risk complaints arrive.
